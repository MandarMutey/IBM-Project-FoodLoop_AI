# 🍲 FoodLoop AI — Smart Food Rescue Platform

**An AI-powered concept to connect surplus food donors with NGOs and volunteers to reduce food waste and help fight hunger.**

## 📌 Project Overview

FoodLoop AI is a student project concept designed to help redirect surplus food from restaurants, events, and other food donors to NGOs and people in need. The platform demonstrates how AI-assisted matching and coordinated delivery could help reduce food waste and support communities.

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Large amounts of edible food are wasted every day while many people face food insecurity. FoodLoop AI explores a digital solution to connect food donors, NGOs, and volunteers for more efficient food redistribution.

## 💡 Proposed Solution

The platform demonstrates a food-rescue workflow involving five conceptual agents:

- **Donor Agent:** Represents people or organizations offering surplus food.
- **NGO Matching Agent:** Helps identify suitable recipient organizations.
- **Volunteer Agent:** Represents volunteers who can assist with collection and delivery.
- **Logistics Agent:** Demonstrates coordination of food pickup and delivery.
- **Confirmation Agent:** Demonstrates delivery status and confirmation.

## ✨ Key Features

- Interactive food-rescue demonstration.
- Sample food donation and recipient matching.
- Volunteer and delivery coordination simulation.
- Delivery status and confirmation workflow.
- Focus on reducing food waste and supporting communities.
